NOTICE

This package contains a local symbol/image generation model derived from SDXS-512 DreamShaper (IDKiro/sdxs-512-dreamshaper), converted to the MNN runtime format.

Original work and components
- Upstream model: IDKiro/sdxs-512-dreamshaper
- Source: https://huggingface.co/IDKiro/sdxs-512-dreamshaper
- UNet and text encoder: SDXS weights (distilled from DreamShaper-8, which declares creativeml-openrail-m on Hugging Face; Stable Diffusion 1.5 lineage).
- VAE: Tiny AutoEncoder for Stable Diffusion (TAESD) by madebyollin, licensed under MIT.
- Text encoder architecture: CLIP ViT-L/14 text tower (OpenAI CLIP), licensed under MIT.

Modifications made in this package
- Exported PyTorch components to ONNX format with diffusion timestep fixed to 999.
- Quantization: {quant_desc}.
- Constructed standard CLIP BPE tokenizer.json from vocab.json and merges.txt.
- Converted ONNX graphs to MNN runtime format.
- Weights were not retrained or fine-tuned; the semantic behavior of the weights was not altered. Outputs of the modified weights may differ slightly from the original model due to numerical precision.
